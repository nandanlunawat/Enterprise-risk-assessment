Varun Beverages Limited – Enterprise Risk Management Assessment

Overview

This project presents an Enterprise Risk Management assessment of Varun Beverages Limited (NSE: VBL).

The project identifies the major risks faced by VBL and evaluates them using probability-impact analysis, causal analysis, influence diagrams, scenario analysis and risk-response planning.

The analysis uses CY2025 audited financial information as the main financial base and considers a 12–24 month risk horizon.

Objectives

The project focuses on:

• Identifying major internal and external risks affecting VBL
• Assessing and prioritising risks using probability and financial impact
• Analysing the causes and effects of major risks
• Examining how risks affect operating and financial outcomes
• Testing the business under adverse scenarios
• Developing risk responses and monitoring indicators
• Identifying selected opportunities linked to the risk environment

Methodology

The project follows the risk management process covered in the course:

1. Establishing the context
2. Risk identification
3. Risk analysis
4. Risk evaluation
5. Risk treatment
6. Monitoring and review
7. Communication and consultation

Probability scale

5 – Very High: >70%
4 – High: 50–69%
3 – Medium: 30–49%
2 – Low: 10–29%
1 – Very Low: <10%

Impact scale

Impact is assessed using annual EBITDA exposure, based on VBL's CY2025 audited EBITDA.

5 – Severe: >₹5,000 mn
4 – Major: ₹2,500–5,000 mn
3 – Moderate: ₹1,000–2,500 mn
2 – Minor: ₹250–1,000 mn
1 – Insignificant: <₹250 mn

Risk score = Probability × Impact

Risk ratings:

1–4: Low
5–9: Medium
10–15: High
16–25: Critical

The probability and impact thresholds are team-defined analytical thresholds because VBL does not publicly disclose a numerical risk appetite or scoring scale.

Risk areas

The broader risk register covers risks across:

• Climate and environment
• Regulatory and compliance
• Financial and market
• Strategic and international
• Operations and supply chain

The five risks selected for detailed analysis are:

1. Peak-season weather variability in India
2. Demerit fiscal treatment of carbonated drinks
3. Competitive intensity and price disruption in India
4. Acquisition integration – Twizza and Bevco
5. PepsiCo franchise concentration and renewal terms

PepsiCo concentration is retained as a headline risk based on its strategic importance and potential severity, despite its lower numerical score.

Project components

Risk Register

The risk register records the identified risks, probability, impact, score, causes, effects, existing controls and recommended actions.

Fishbone Analysis

Fishbone analysis is used to identify the main causes and effects associated with the risks.

Probability-Impact Matrix

A 5×5 probability-impact matrix is used to compare and prioritise the identified risks.

Influence Diagrams

Influence diagrams are used for the five major risks to show decision variables, uncertain events, calculated outcomes and the final value or business outcome.

Scenario Analysis

An illustrative stress test compares:

• CY2025 Actual
• Base case
• Adverse case
• Severe case

The analysis considers volume, revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA margin and PAT.

The scenario analysis is based on team assumptions and is not a company forecast.

Risk Responses and Monitoring

The project proposes responses and monitoring indicators for the major risks using the treatment categories:

• Reduce
• Retain
• Remove
• Transfer

Key findings

The analysis highlights the interaction between Indian demand and weather conditions, pricing pressure, regulatory constraints and operating leverage.

International expansion provides diversification but also increases exposure to integration, country and currency risks.
